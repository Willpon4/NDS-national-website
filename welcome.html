01 / Welcome — new member intake

Welcome to the National Defense Society

Membership is open to enrolled undergraduates at any of the 10 chapter campuses. No major requirement. No dues in your first semester.

Action 01
02 / Procedure — how membership works3 steps · est. 1 semester
02.1
Apply

A five-minute form, below. Your chapter lead reviews it within seven days and invites you to the next meeting.

02.2
Attend

Two events in your first semester. Speaker nights, a briefing drill, or the winter tabletop exercise.

02.3
Commit

Full membership after one semester: spring placement cycle, fellowship eligibility, and a vote in chapter elections.
