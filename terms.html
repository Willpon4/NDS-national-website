02 / Who we are

The National Defense Society ("NDS", "the Society") is an Indiana nonprofit corporation recognized as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, headquartered in West Lafayette, Indiana.

These terms apply to the website and to participation in chapter and national programs.

03 / Membership

Membership is open to undergraduates enrolled at a campus with a chartered chapter. Applications are reviewed by student officers. The Society may decline or revoke membership for conduct inconsistent with its code, described in Section 4.

Membership confers no employment, placement, or security-clearance guarantee. Partner firms make their own hiring decisions.

04 / Accounts and communications

Providing an email address to apply or donate authorizes NDS to contact you about that request.

You are responsible for the accuracy of information you submit.

05 / Code of conduct

Members and visitors to NDS events agree not to harass, discriminate, or misrepresent themselves or the Society. Members may not present personal views as positions of NDS.

Classified or export-controlled information may not be discussed at any NDS event or in any NDS publication.
